# My Favorite Series: Agent Kim Reactivated

> A father will do anything to bring his daughter home safely.

## About the Series

**Agent Kim Reactivated** (Korean title: *Kim Bu-jang*) is a 2026 Korean *action spy* series on Netflix and SBS. It is adapted from the webtoon *Manager Kim*.

It follows **Manager Kim**, an ordinary office worker who is secretly a former black-ops agent. When his daughter goes missing, he uses his old skills to find her.

---

## Main Characters

1. Kim Do-hyun (Manager Kim) played by So Ji-sub
2. Kim Min-ji played by Seo Su-min
3. Sung Han-soo played by Choi Dae-hoon
4. Park Jin-cheol played by Yoon Kyung-ho
